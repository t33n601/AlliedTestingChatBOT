# New tester onboarding
Every new tester at the KB Helper demo lab gets a buddy for the first 30 days. On day one they receive a laptop, access to Jira and a purple unicorn sticker. The onboarding checklist has 12 items and must be signed by the buddy.

# Bug bounty for interns
Interns get a 50 euro voucher for every critical bug they find in production systems. The record holder is an intern named Alex, who found 7 critical bugs in March.

# Friday demo
Every Friday at 15:00 the QA team shows one automated test they wrote that week. The best demo of the month wins a mechanical keyboard.
