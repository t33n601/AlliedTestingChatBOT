> how it works

Three steps,
all on screen.

Nothing is hidden. While it works, you watch each step light up — and when it's done you can see exactly what it read.

  1. 01

    You ask

    In plain English or Russian. No keywords, no special syntax — just your question.

  2. 02

    It finds the right pages

    It searches by meaning, not exact words, and picks the five passages that fit your question best.

  3. 03

    It answers and cites

    The answer is written from those passages only, with a numbered link on every claim.
